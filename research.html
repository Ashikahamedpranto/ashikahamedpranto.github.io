Research

Research interests and what's active right now.

Interests

My research sits at the intersection of AI transparency and natural language processing, with three connected threads:

Bias detection in low-resource languages. Most NLP bias research stops at English. I extend gender-bias measurement methods (word-embedding-based and DirectBias-style approaches) to Bengali and Hindi, comparing bias across Wikipedia, literature, newspaper, and translated corpora.

Human–AI collaboration on Wikipedia. I study how AI involvement shows up in Wikipedia edits — building frameworks to detect neutral-point-of-view violations, and to place edits on a spectrum between fully human and fully automated rather than treating that as a binary.

Language resource equity. Through the LEUM (Language Evaluation and Underrepresentation Metric) project, I help measure how well NLP and digital resources support different languages, using survey data collected across 16+ languages so far.

Currently

Awaiting decisions on two Bengali/Hindi gender-bias papers (GeBNLP 2026, WiNLP 2026), preparing the human-vs-bot editing spectrum paper for arXiv, and looking for a venue for both the LEUM metric paper and the "Beyond Wikipedia" corpus study.
