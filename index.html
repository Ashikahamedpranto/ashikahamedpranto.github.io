Biography

I'm a PhD student in the COSI Lab at Clarkson University, advised by Professor Jeanna Matthews, also completing an MS in Computer Science alongside the PhD. My work sits at the intersection of AI transparency and NLP: measuring gender bias across languages and corpora, tracing where AI involvement shows up in Wikipedia edits, and building tools that make that involvement visible rather than invisible.

A lot of this research is deliberately multilingual — much of NLP bias work stops at English, and a good share of mine is built around extending it to Bengali and Hindi, languages that are comparatively under-measured. I'm originally from Narayanganj, Bangladesh, and currently based in Potsdam, New York.
